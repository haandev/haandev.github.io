First, what the user writes: SELECT name, salary FROM employee WHERE salary > 50000. Second, what the two rules produce: the projected salary becomes a LEFT JOIN to employee$salary on the same id with valid containing the effective point, and the predicate becomes an EXISTS subquery on the same side table. A query that does not mention salary never touches the side table.">
